My Experince

One of the most memorable experiences in my first year of school was learning how to create a website for the first time. At the beginning, I felt nervous because I had never learned coding before. However, I was also excited to study something new and useful for the future. Learning website development became an interesting experience for me because it was the beginning of my journey in programming and technology.

During the first lesson, my teacher introduced us to basic website programming languages such as HTML and CSS. My teacher explained how websites are created and how different codes can change the appearance of a webpage. At first, I thought coding was very difficult because there were many tags, commands, and rules that I had to remember. Sometimes I became confused when the code did not work properly. After that, I started practicing in the computer laboratory with my classmates. I tried to create a simple webpage containing my name, hobbies, favorite colors, and pictures. In the beginning, I often made mistakes, such as forgetting closing tags, writing incorrect code, or placing elements in the wrong position. Because of those mistakes, my website sometimes looked messy or could not be opened correctly. I felt frustrated several times, but my teacher patiently helped me understand the errors.

Besides learning at school, I also watched tutorial videos on the internet and practiced at home. I spent a lot of time trying different designs and colors to make my website look more attractive. Slowly, I began to understand how HTML and CSS work together. I learned how to add text, images, backgrounds, and simple layouts to a webpage. After several weeks of learning and practicing, I finally succeeded in creating my first simple website. The website contained my personal profile, hobbies, favorite music, and some photos. When I saw the final result on the computer screen, I felt very happy and proud of myself because it was the first website I had ever made independently.

From this experience, I learned that creating a website requires patience, creativity, and hard work. Even though I faced many difficulties at the beginning, I did not give up and continued learning. This experience became one of the most unforgettable moments in my school life because it was the start of my journey in programming and website development.
